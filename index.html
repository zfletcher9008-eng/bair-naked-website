    The science

    Why copper?

    GHK-Cu is a small peptide bound to copper. Your body makes it naturally, and it has been studied for decades for how it supports the look of firm, smooth, resilient skin.

    Peptides are delicate. We never heat ours. It is dissolved separately and folded in by hand once each batch has cooled below 40 °C, then packed in matte black airless pumps, droppers and jars that keep out light.

    Our scalp mist pairs it with AHK-Cu, a sister copper peptide used in hair care, plus caffeine and rosemary.

    The ritual

    How to layer it.

    Thin to thick. Water-light serum first, then cream to seal it in. Keep vitamin C products for a different time of day than your copper peptides.

    i.Morning & night

    Cleanse

    Start on clean skin, patted almost dry.

    ii.Morning & night

    Face Serum

    Press 2–3 drops of the 1% GHK-Cu serum into face and neck.

    iii.Morning & night

    Seal

    1–2 pumps of Barrier & Eye Cream, a dab under the eyes. At night, the Emu & Tallow cream for extra richness.

    iv.Once or twice a week

    Polish

    Massage the Sugar Scrub onto damp skin, then rinse.
